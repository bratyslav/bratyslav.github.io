The Balkan Boom: Albania’s Property Surge

From Tirana’s skyline to the Riviera, prices are reaching new heights in 2026

April 29, 2026 Jane Doe

Albania’s real estate market has transformed from a regional secret into a full-blown European hotspot. In early 2026, the country continues to witness double-digit growth, with average property values climbing between 15% and 25% over the past twelve months. Central Tirana remains the powerhouse of this surge, where premium districts like Blloku and the Artificial Lake area see prices reaching as high as €5,000 per square meter for luxury new-builds. However, the real excitement is shifting toward the "Albanian Riviera" as coastal cities prepare for a historic infrastructure boom.
